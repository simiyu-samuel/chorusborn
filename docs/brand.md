# Name and creative direction

## Chosen working name: Chorusborn

Pronunciation: **KOR-us-born**. Repository slug: `chorusborn`.

**Tagline:** Raised by chat. Proven in the arena.

**Short descriptor:** Community-taught champions for Twitch.

"Chorus" evokes many individual voices making something together. "Born" gives the shared champion an identity that belongs to its community. The name supports both affectionate attachment and competitive play without describing the product as a generic assistant.

A preliminary exact-name web search on October 26, 2026 did not surface an obvious matching software product. This is not trademark clearance or a domain-availability check. No domain has been purchased. Hivemind remains a historical concept name only; use Chorusborn throughout new work.

## Brand language

Use: champion, coach, lesson, playbook, trial, rivalry, rematch, contribution.

Examples: "Teach your champion", "Prove this lesson", "Adopt for next round", "See what changed", "Lesson used: carry the core home".

Explain actual behavior rather than human-like consciousness. Say "The champion used your lesson" when a recorded rule fired. Do not say "Your lesson won the match" without evidence, "the AI understands everything", or "we train a new foundation model".

## Visual direction

An energetic broadcast sports identity with a warm, creature-like champion. Deep ink backgrounds, ivory text, electric violet for community A, amber for community B, and teal for trial evidence. Team labels, shapes, and patterns must distinguish teams without color alone.

The champion has a recognizable silhouette, expressive idle/win/loss reactions, and a small set of cosmetics. Keep collision boxes identical across cosmetics. Avoid a dashboard dominating the broadcast: the arena and visible consequences are the hero.

Use short lesson cards, clear replay timelines, directional intent cues, and restrained particle effects. Protect OBS frame rate. A proposed arena/character concept and production brief now exist in [art direction](art-direction.md). The current app has a shared Three.js renderer, original procedural champions, self-hosted fonts and SVG compatibility art. The slender fighter kit now renders in live training, branch replays and the overlay; its complete-replay choreography remains inspectable in [Combat Lab](combat-direction.md); see third-party notices. Final logo, production art consistency, and runtime asset licensing/provenance checks remain release work.
